AI writing tools have reached the point where they can produce personalized, natural-sounding review request messages in seconds — and for contractors running high job volume, that capability is genuinely useful. The challenge is understanding what AI actually changes (message quality and personalization at scale) versus what it doesn't change (the compliance framework that governs when and how review requests are sent).

This article explains what AI-assisted review request generation looks like in practice, where the real value is, and how to use it without crossing into compliance issues.

## What AI actually does in review request generation

<AnswerBox>
AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, and built-in AI features in review platforms) generate the text of review request messages. They can personalize based on job type, trade, customer name, and project details — producing messages that feel individual rather than templated. What AI doesn't do: determine who gets a request, decide the timing, or change the compliance requirements. Those are still your decisions.
</AnswerBox>

The two primary use cases for AI in review requests:

**1. Personalized message drafting at scale**

Instead of sending every customer the same template, AI enables you to vary the message based on job type, trade, location, or other inputs. Example:

*Generic template:* "Hi [Name], thanks for your business. Please leave us a Google review: [link]."

*AI-personalized version:* "Hi Sarah — really glad the new HVAC system is running well before the summer heat kicks in. If our team took good care of you through the install, a quick Google review would mean a lot: [link]. Takes about 30 seconds."

The second message names the job, references the customer's immediate benefit, and feels like it was written by a person who knows the job — because it was written with that specific context in mind.

**2. Review response drafting**

AI is equally useful on the response side. When a new Google review comes in, an AI tool can generate a draft response in seconds — with the right tone, appropriate acknowledgment of specifics, and a professional close. You review and approve before posting. This turns a 5-minute task per review into a 30-second one.

## Compliance: what AI changes and what it doesn't

<AnswerBox>
Using AI to draft review request messages does not create any new compliance risk under Google's review policies. The compliance rules (no gating, no incentivizing, ask everyone the same way) govern the system design — not the tool used to write the message. A gating funnel is a violation whether the messages are written by AI or a human. An honest, universal ask is compliant whether AI wrote it or you did.
</AnswerBox>

Where AI could introduce risk: if an AI tool includes gating language by default. Some AI-generated templates include phrases like "If you're happy with the service..." or "For customers who had a great experience..." — these are soft gating and should be removed. Review every AI-generated message against this rule: does the message include any conditional language that implies only satisfied customers should respond?

The compliant version asks everyone, unconditionally: "We'd love your feedback on Google." The non-compliant version screens: "If you're happy, we'd love a review." AI can write both — it's your job to specify the compliance requirement and review the output.

## Using AI to personalize by contractor trade and job type

Here's how to prompt an AI tool to generate compliant, trade-specific review request messages:

**Prompt structure:**
*"Write a short, natural SMS review request for a roofing contractor. The homeowner's name is [Name]. The job was a full shingle replacement that completed today. The message should ask for a Google review, include a placeholder for the link, feel personal and not templated, and not include any conditional language (no 'if you're happy' phrasing). Max 160 characters."*

**Example output:**
*"Hi [Name] — glad the new roof is looking solid. If you have 30 sec, a Google review means a lot to a local company: [link]. Thanks!"*

This is compliant, personal, specific to the job type, and under 160 characters for SMS. The same prompt structure works for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and GC jobs — swap the job specifics.

## Manual vs. AI-assisted review request workflow comparison

| Element | Manual workflow | AI-assisted workflow |
|---|---|---|
| Message quality | Consistent template, impersonal | Personalized per job type and customer |
| Time per request | 2–5 min (drafting + sending) | Under 1 min with AI draft |
| Compliance control | Owner-controlled | Owner-controlled (review AI output) |
| Scalability | Breaks down above 20 jobs/week | Scales to any volume |
| Cost | $0 | $0–$300/month depending on tool |
| Conversion rate | Baseline | 15–30% higher with personalization |

The manual workflow is the right starting point — build the system, confirm it works, then add AI personalization when volume justifies it. Don't invest in a $300/month AI platform before you've proven the basic ask system converts.
