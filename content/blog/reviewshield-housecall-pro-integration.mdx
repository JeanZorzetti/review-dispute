## Setting up review requests in Housecall Pro

**Plan requirement:** Housecall Pro Grow ($169/month) or above. Basic plan users need to upgrade or use a third-party platform.

**Step 1: Access review settings**
Go to Settings → Reviews in your Housecall Pro dashboard. Enable the review request feature.

**Step 2: Connect your Google Business Profile**
Housecall Pro requires a connected GBP to automatically use your review link. Connect your profile under Settings → Integrations → Google Business Profile.

**Step 3: Configure the trigger and timing**
- Trigger: Invoice paid
- Delay: 24 hours (recommended — gives customer time to settle and reduces "too soon" friction)
- Follow-up: 4 days after first message if no review posted

**Step 4: Write your message template**
Housecall Pro provides a default template. Customize it to match your brand voice:
```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for letting [Company Name] take care of your [service type] today.
If you have 30 seconds, leaving a Google review helps other homeowners find us:

[Google Review Link]

Thanks so much — [Your Name]
```

**Step 5: Test with a real job**
Create a test customer, invoice, and payment event. Verify:
- Message arrives within the configured delay
- Google review link resolves correctly on mobile
- Follow-up fires at day 4–5 if the test account doesn't review

## Compliance verification

Housecall Pro's native review request is compliant as configured — it sends to all paid-invoice customers without filtering by satisfaction score.

**One risk point:** If you use any Housecall Pro integration with a reputation management platform that adds a pre-review survey, check whether that survey routes customers to different destinations based on their score. If unhappy customers are being sent to a private form while happy ones go to Google, that's gating — a violation regardless of which platform executes it.

**The compliant configuration:** invoice paid → review request to all customers → Google link for everyone → no satisfaction-score routing.

## Using Housecall Pro records to verify reviews

When a suspicious review appears on your Google profile, Housecall Pro's customer and job records are your verification tool:

**Verification steps:**
1. Search customers by first/last name matching the review
2. Search by phone number if name doesn't match
3. Check job history for the approximate date of the review — does a matching job exist?
4. Compare service type in the review against job records

If no matching customer or job exists in Housecall Pro, you have a strong starting point for a ReviewShield removal case. Export the relevant search results as evidence before initiating the case.

## When to open a ReviewShield case from your Housecall Pro workflow

- A review appears from a name with no matching Housecall Pro customer record
- Review content describes a service or date that doesn't match any job in your records
- Multiple suspicious reviews appear in a cluster within 48–72 hours
- Review contains language that violates Google's content policies (threats, off-topic content, hate speech)

**What to bring to a ReviewShield case:**
- Screenshot of the Google review (include the reviewer's name and date)
- Housecall Pro search results showing no matching customer
- Any other context (competitor activity, recent dispute with a non-customer)
