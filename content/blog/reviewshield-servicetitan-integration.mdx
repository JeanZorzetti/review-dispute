## What ServiceTitan Marketing Pro includes for review requests

ServiceTitan Marketing Pro's "After-Job Communication" feature allows you to configure automated messages that fire when a job reaches a specific status — typically "Complete" or "Closed." This is the core review request trigger.

**What's included:**
- Automated SMS and email review requests from completed jobs
- Template builder for message content (personalized with customer name, job type)
- Follow-up sequences if the first message doesn't convert
- Google Business Profile integration for review link delivery
- Reporting on message open rates and review response

**What requires Marketing Pro:**
This is an add-on to ServiceTitan's base platform, typically adding ~$200–$350/month. If you're on ServiceTitan but not Marketing Pro, you'll need either the Marketing Pro upgrade or a separate review platform.

## Setting up the review request automation in ServiceTitan

**Step 1: Access Marketing Pro automation**
In your ServiceTitan dashboard, navigate to Marketing → Automations → New Automation.

**Step 2: Configure the trigger**
- Trigger type: Job status change
- Status: "Complete" (or whatever job-complete status your operation uses)
- Filter: You can filter by job type, business unit, or technician if you want to narrow the scope

**Step 3: Set the delay**
- First message: 24 hours after job completion (gives the homeowner time to settle before the ask)
- Second follow-up: 4–5 days after first message if no review posted

**Step 4: Write the message template**
Use ServiceTitan's merge fields to personalize:
```
Hi {{customer.first_name}}, this is {{technician.name}} from {{company.name}}.
Thank you for choosing us for your {{job.job_type}} today.
If you have 30 seconds, a Google review would mean a lot: {{google_review_link}}
Thanks either way!
```

**Step 5: Configure compliance settings**
- Do NOT enable satisfaction-score routing (this creates gating — a Google policy violation)
- Set the sequence to send to ALL completed-job customers, not filtered by survey score
- Verify the review link resolves correctly on both iOS and Android before activating

**Step 6: Test**
Create a test job, complete it, and verify the automation fires correctly. Check that:
- The text arrives within the configured delay window
- The review link opens the correct Google review form
- The follow-up fires at the right interval

## Monitoring your review pipeline in ServiceTitan

ServiceTitan Marketing Pro provides reporting on:
- Messages sent vs. delivered
- Open rates by channel (SMS vs. email)
- Review conversion rate by job type or business unit

Use these reports monthly to check system health. A sudden drop in conversion rate (reviews per completed job) signals either a broken automation, a link issue, or a change in job volume. Investigate before it compounds.

## Where ReviewShield fits

ServiceTitan Marketing Pro handles the generation side of your review strategy — sending requests, following up, and tracking conversion. ReviewShield handles the protection side: identifying and removing reviews that violate Google's content policy.

**When to open a ReviewShield case:**

1. A one-star review appears from a name not in your ServiceTitan job records
2. Multiple negative reviews appear within a short window from accounts with no review history
3. A review contains language that violates Google's policies (threats, off-topic content, competitor impersonation)
4. Your ServiceTitan job history confirms the reviewer never received service from you

**The process:**
1. Screenshot the review immediately
2. Cross-reference against ServiceTitan customer records (customer name, address, phone)
3. If no match is found, initiate a ReviewShield case with the review URL, screenshot, and ServiceTitan confirmation of no matching customer
4. ReviewShield builds the evidence file and submits to Google

ReviewShield charges **$499 per removed review, pay-on-removal** — if the review doesn't come down, you owe nothing.
